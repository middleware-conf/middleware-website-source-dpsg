MIDDLEWARE recognizes outstanding work with three awards:

- **[Test of Time Award](/awards/test-of-time/)**: given since 2009 to a paper published in MIDDLEWARE ten years earlier that has had the most impact since publication.
- **[Best Paper Award](/awards/best-paper/)**: selected by the Program Committee of each conference, with runners-up recognized since 2022.
- **[Best Student Paper Award](/awards/best-student-paper/)**: selected by the Program Committee of each conference, with runners-up recognized since 2025.

The 2026 awards will be announced at [MIDDLEWARE 2026](https://middleware-conf.github.io/2026/) in Tarragona.
